# Privacy Policy — Robo Sims

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

Robo Sims does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server we control. We operate no server, and the app makes no network requests.

## What the app stores
Settings and progress, if any, are kept in local app storage on your device. Nothing is synced anywhere by the app.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

## Permissions
The app requests no permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or notifications.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Changes
Material changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.
